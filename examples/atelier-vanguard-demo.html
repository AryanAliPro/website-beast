The 4-Phase Atelier Methodology

Our proven protocol eliminates remodeling delays, cost inflation, and contractor friction through unified architectural governance.

PHASE 01

Spatial Audit & 3D Photogrammetry

Millimeter-precise LiDAR scanning of existing structural frames, load-bearing verification, mechanical routing feasibility, and daylight path analysis.

Deliverable: 3D Digital Twin & Permit Pack
PHASE 02

Material Curation & Engineering

Direct quarry reserve tagging, structural steel calculation, custom millwork shop drawings, and complete fixed-bracket investment locking.

Deliverable: 100% Fixed-Price Commission Contract
PHASE 03

Guild Craft & Site Governance

Execution conducted strictly by our licensed master carpenters, stonemasons, and engineers under a full-time resident guild foreman.

Deliverable: Daily Client Portal Live Feeds
PHASE 04

White-Glove Commissioning

Comprehensive HVAC balancing, smart home acoustic tuning, deep architectural cleaning, and issuance of the 15-year structural transferrable warranty.

Deliverable: Bespoke Owner's Manual & Warranty Deed
